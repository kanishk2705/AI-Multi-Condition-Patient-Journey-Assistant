About this tool

What it is for

Reliable patient information is usually written one condition at a time. People living with both asthma and type 2 diabetes have to piece answers together across many pages. This tool searches a fixed set of 373 passages from four U.S. government health websites, writes a short answer only from the passages it found, and shows exactly where each sentence came from.

What it will not do

It does not give medicine doses, does not tell you to start, stop or change a medicine, and cannot tell you whether you have a condition. If a question sounds like an emergency it shows an urgent-care message instead of an answer. It was tested on questions written by its developer, not by patients or clinicians, and no clinician has reviewed it.

Privacy

No cookies, analytics or tracking. Your question is sent to the answer service and to the language model provider (Groq) to write the answer; it is not stored by this project. Fonts are served from this site.
